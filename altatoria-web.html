Founding Principle

1987

Altatoria is a privately held global holding company, founded on the conviction that enduring value cannot be hurried. We do not manage funds. We do not seek liquidity events.

We acquire exceptional businesses and hold them indefinitely — not as a strategy, but as a philosophy. Our capital is patient because our ownership is permanent.

Headquartered across Geneva and London, Altatoria serves a singular mandate: to compound capital across generations without compromise.

Discipline  ·  Compounding  ·  Permanence

Our Convictions

Three principles,
held without revision.

I
Permanence of Capital

We do not exit. Our investments are held indefinitely, across economic cycles — giving management the rarest gift in modern finance: the freedom to think in decades.

II
Quality Without Compromise

We acquire only businesses of genuine distinction — characterised by irreplaceable assets, structural pricing power, and operators of exceptional character.

III
Disciplined Restraint

We say no far more than we say yes. Capital is deployed rarely, deliberately, and only when conviction is unambiguous and alignment complete.
